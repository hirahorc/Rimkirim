Cargo and courier can both get your belongings from one place to another, but the two services work quite differently — in shipping method, volume handled, and what kind of sender they're built for.

So when should you use courier, and when does cargo make more sense? Understanding the difference makes it easier to pick the service that actually fits what you're shipping.

## What Is Courier?

Courier is a shipping service typically used for sending documents or individual packages, with a process built around speed and simplicity. It covers a wide range of needs, from documents and personal parcels to items bought online.

Because it's designed around handling individual packages, courier tends to be the better fit when you're not shipping much — and you need something straightforward.

## What Is Cargo?

Cargo, on the other hand, is built to handle shipments with a larger quantity, weight, or volume. It's commonly used for business needs, product distribution, and relocation shipments.

For international relocation specifically, the amount being shipped can range from a few boxes to several hundred kilograms. That's what makes cargo the more practical option when you're moving a large volume of belongings at once. If you're in the middle of planning your move back to Indonesia, understanding how cargo works is a good place to start before you get into costs and paperwork.

## Cargo vs Courier: What's the Difference?

| | Courier | Cargo |
|---|---|---|
| **Shipment type** | Documents and individual packages | Larger quantity or volume of goods |
| **Quantity** | Usually one or a few packages | Can range from several to many packages |
| **Typical use** | Personal shipping, documents, e-commerce | Relocation, retail distribution, business needs |
| **Weight & volume** | Generally lighter | Can handle large-weight/volume shipments |
| **Shipping approach** | Focused on individual packages | Focused on the shipment as a whole |
| **Example** | A document or single package | A relocation shipment of several boxes to hundreds of kg |

## When Should You Use Courier?

Courier makes sense when you only need to send a few items or packages individually and don't need to move a large volume. Think documents, personal parcels, e-commerce purchases, or other smaller items.

## When Should You Use Cargo?

Cargo is the better fit once what you're shipping starts adding up in volume or weight — especially if it's several boxes or multiple items you want sent together.

This is the case if you're moving house, relocating an office, or moving back to Indonesia after living abroad. Instead of shipping items one at a time, cargo lets everything go as a single shipment, which keeps the whole process more organized.

## How Do You Decide Which Service Is Right?

Consider:

- How much are you shipping?
- What's the total weight and volume?
- Is this part of a larger relocation?
- Do you need door-to-door pickup and delivery?
- How quickly does it need to arrive?

If what you're shipping is part of a move and the volume is substantial, cargo is usually the more practical choice.
