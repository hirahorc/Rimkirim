# Expat customers never pick a Customs Path on the web

In the Expat Relocation flow the customer explains their move (the Eligibility Check) but never chooses how their belongings clear customs, the opposite of Back For Good, where the customer picks Personal Belongings or Passenger Goods. The answers only set a provisional internal Customs Category; at review, ops recommends the most favourable Customs Path the customer's documents allow (Relocation Goods, Passenger Goods or Personal Shipment), asks for anything missing, and the customer decides. The web explains all three paths and their documents equally, on the landing page and inside the flow, so the customer arrives at review informed. Decided with the PM: customs depends on documents a form can't verify, and a wrong self-selection costs more to unwind than a conversation.

## Consequences

- The Customs Category is never rendered to the customer, in the flow, on the Booking Summary or in the confirmation email.
- Any customs information shown in the flow is the same for everyone, never tailored to the customer's answers, so it can't reveal the category.
