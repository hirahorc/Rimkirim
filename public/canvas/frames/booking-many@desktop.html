What happens next

  1. 1

    We review your booking

    Our team reviews what's in your packages and flags anything that can't be sent or is likely to be taxed. Within 1 working day, we contact you on WhatsApp and email: we recommend the way through customs that suits you best and, once you've chosen, complete its documents and your packing list with you.

  2. 2

    You get your quotation

    An estimate of your shipping cost, before anything goes ahead.

  3. 3

    We pick up your belongings

    On Thursday, 26 November 2026, at the address you're shipping from. Have everything packed by then.

Your invoice comes later, once your belongings have arrived at our warehouse in Indonesia. It shows your final price.

There are three ways in, each with the documents it needs.

  • Relocation goods (Barang Pindahan)

    For:
    Moving to Indonesia on a work or study ITAS
    Duty and tax:
    Duty-free, no value limit

    Documents needed

    • Passport
    • ITAS for work or study, valid 12 months or more
    • Your work approval or study permit
    • Commercial invoice/packing list

  • Passenger goods (Barang Penumpang)

    For:
    Arriving in Indonesia, with your goods sent separately
    Duty and tax:
    Free up to USD 500

    Documents needed

    • Passport
    • Your arrival date and flight details
    • Commercial invoice/packing list

  • Personal shipment (Barang Kiriman Pribadi)

    For:
    Anyone, any time
    Duty and tax:
    Duty and tax apply

    Documents needed

    • Your passport or NPWP
    • Commercial invoice/packing list

  • What's a commercial invoice/packing list?

    A list of everything you're sending, package by package, with what each item is worth. You create it yourself on Rimkirim's packing list page.

    Read more in the FAQ

Customs may occasionally ask for additional documents. We'll let you know if they do.

No need to choose now. When we review your booking, we'll point out the one that saves you the most and what it needs. The choice is yours.
